justacamera.app

Webcam & Microphone Test — Private, In Your Browser

Test your webcam and microphone, take photos, and record video — entirely in your browser. Your photos, videos, audio, and camera frames never leave your device.

Most online webcam tests are full of ads and ask you to trust that they aren’t keeping your video. justacamera.app removes the need for trust: there is no server to send anything to, and the code is free, open source, and auditable. It works in current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari (including iOS) over HTTPS.

What you can do

  • View a live webcam preview and select your camera and microphone.
  • Switch between front and rear cameras.
  • Take photos (JPEG, PNG or WebP) with a countdown and mirror controls.
  • Record video in your browser, with pause, resume and instant playback.
  • Save photos and recordings locally — there is no upload.
  • Use advanced controls (zoom, torch, focus, exposure) where your device supports them.
  • Open a privacy diagnostics panel with a sanitized, shareable report.

How it works

  1. Click start and allow camera access when your browser asks.
  2. See your live webcam preview and pick your camera and microphone.
  3. Take photos or record video — everything stays in memory on your device.
  4. Download what you want to keep; close the tab and the rest is discarded.

Private by design — and provable

  • No server, no uploads, no accounts, no cookies, and no analytics.
  • Open source under AGPL-3.0; automated tests enforce the no-upload promise.
  • Verify it live: open your browser’s Network tab and you will see no requests while you use the camera.

Frequently asked questions

How do I test my webcam?

Start the camera test and allow access when your browser asks. Your live webcam feed appears instantly. Nothing is recorded or uploaded unless you choose to capture and download it.

How do I test my microphone online?

Start the test and choose your microphone from the device list. The app confirms the microphone is active, and you can record a short clip and play it back. Your audio never leaves your device.

Is justacamera.app private? Do you upload my video or photos?

No. There is no server and no upload. Every operation runs locally in your browser, and your photos, videos, audio, and camera frames never leave your device. Because the app is open source, you can verify this in your browser’s Network tab.

Can I record video in my browser without installing anything?

Yes. You can record video, optionally with microphone audio, entirely in the browser, then play it back and download it locally. No account, no install, nothing uploaded.

Why is my webcam not working?

Check that you allowed camera permission, that no other application is using the camera, and that the page is loaded over HTTPS. justacamera.app shows a specific message for denied, busy, missing, unsupported, or constrained devices.

Does it work on Windows, macOS, Android and iPhone?

Yes. It works in current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, including iOS, over HTTPS. Advanced camera controls appear where the device and browser support them.

Is justacamera.app free and open source?

Yes. It is free and open source under the AGPL-3.0 license. You can read and audit the full source code on GitHub.

Can I use it to test my camera for Zoom, Teams or Google Meet?

Yes. Use it as a quick pre-call check of your webcam and microphone before joining Zoom, Microsoft Teams or Google Meet, or before going live on Twitch or OBS.
